## Honest stand-ins

- Wooden spinning top and whip is not labeled as an Indian lattu.
- Crown caps are not shown being played, and the file does not name India. They fill the empty tape-ball slot and are captioned as caps.
- Lacquer tiffin carrier is not an Indian steel dabba.
- Masala dabba is a spice box, not a lunch tiffin.
- Unlit sparkler was photographed in Virginia.
- Hopscotch photo does not say India.
- Glass marbles on the U.S. shelf do not state a country.
- Jianlibao picture is a modern shop display, not a vintage glass bottle.
- Dehong enamel mugs are slogan mugs, not floral.
- Bee & Flower photograph was taken in British Columbia.
- Painted tin buckets are not a plain galvanized pail.
- Trompo and balero share a frame with a yo-yo; country not stated.
- Bungeoppang picture is the pastry in Toronto, not the mold.
- Uludağ Gazoz bottles are described as sold in the United Kingdom.
- Karagöz picture is a shadow at a Seattle festival.

## Left out on purpose

- Tape ball and brick wicket, Sakuma tin, iron hoop, tin clicker frog, floral enamel mug, Subor (the only hardware photo had a large anime mat behind it), Little Raccoon noodles, jacks, plain pail, plain pogs, candy buttons, school milk caps, empty gashapon, lead menko, lit fireworks, film posters, Nintendo and NES, character lunchboxes.
- Camlin, Rasna, kanche as a specifically Indian marble photo, patang without manjha, lotería deck, paleta, festa junina flags, pião, peteca, ddakji, hwatu, faceted glass, Soviet siphon, eskimo bar, New Year bauble as a single plain ornament, topaç, lokum tin, bottle-cap football, groundnut twist, ankara fold, unlit lamp with a Nigeria caption.
- Germany, Indonesia, Philippines, and the UK were not built. Pakistan is not a separate shelf.
